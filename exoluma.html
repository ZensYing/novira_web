The Science

Tiny vesicles. Big cellular messages.

Exosomes are nano-sized vesicles that carry biological signals between cells — proteins, lipids and genetic material wrapped in a protective membrane. EXOLUMA supplies them in a freeze-dried powder, paired with an active solution.
