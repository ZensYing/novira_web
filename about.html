Dermatology & Aesthetic Distribution

Pioneering Aesthetic Medicine Across Cambodia

NOVIRA is a dermatology and aesthetic distribution company based in Cambodia. The company focuses on importing and distributing high-quality products and technologies related to skin health and aesthetic medicine.

NOVIRA aims to become a trusted partner for dermatologists, aesthetic doctors, clinics, and healthcare providers by delivering innovative solutions that improve skin health and aesthetic outcomes.
