Why JUVELOOK is Revolutionizing Aesthetic Biostimulation

Traditional biostimulators carry risks of subcutaneous nodules due to irregular, jagged crystal geometries. JUVELOOK solves this through patented round porous micro-particle architecture.

Patented Porous Spherical Micro-Particles

“Unlike older PLLA particles with sharp needle-like edges that provoke aggressive foreign body granulomas, JUVELOOK’s PDLLA particles are engineered as perfect porous micro-spheres. Macrophages safely penetrate the particle pores, producing gentle, sustained fibroblast recruitment without nodule formation.”
  • ✓ Safe Under Delicate Skin: The only biostimulator certified for thin periorbital skin, tear troughs, and neck bands.
  • ✓ Rapid Dissolution & Reconstitution: Reconstitutes completely with saline in under 15 minutes without clumping.
  • ✓ Biodegradable Clearance: Hydrolyzes naturally into harmless H2O and CO2 via Krebs cycle metabolism over 24 months.

Dual-Action Hybrid: PDLLA + Hyaluronic Acid

“By combining high-purity non-crosslinked Hyaluronic Acid with Poly-D,L-Lactic Acid, JUVELOOK provides immediate tissue micro-hydration upon injection while simultaneously initiating a 2-year cascade of endogenous Type I and Type III collagen synthesis.”
  • ✓ Phase 1 (Day 1–14): HA carrier instantly plumps fine lines, hydrates dermal ECM, and cushions particle dispersion.
  • ✓ Phase 2 (Month 1–24): Porous PDLLA activates fibroblasts, progressively replacing the resorbable matrix with autologous collagen.
  • ✓ Natural Dynamic Expression: Enhances structural skin quality and dermal elasticity without looking over-filled or stiff.
