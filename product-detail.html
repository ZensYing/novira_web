Practitioner Guide

Standardized Clinical Reconstitution & Administration

Follow strict aseptic medical protocols to maintain exosomal membrane viability and ensure optimal clinical results.

4-Step Reconstitution Protocol

1
Aseptic Vial Unsealing

Disinfect the rubber stoppers of Vial 1 (Exosome Lyophilized Cake) and Vial 2 (PDRN + Solvent) with 70% isopropyl alcohol.

2
Solvent Aspiration & Transfer

Using a sterile 18G reconstitution needle and 5ml syringe, aspirate the entire contents of Vial 2 and slowly inject along the inner wall of Vial 1.

3
Gentle Dissolution (No Vortexing)

Gently swirl the vial in circular motions for 30–60 seconds until completely clear. Do not shake vigorously to avoid shear stress on exosome vesicles.

4
Administration Window

Deliver immediately or within 3 hours of reconstitution when stored at 2°C – 8°C. One reconstituted set yields approximately 5.0ml of active serum.
